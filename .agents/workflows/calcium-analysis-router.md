# Calcium Analysis Router

Purpose

Route work related to experiment loading, stimulus alignment, response filtering and classification, significance detection, single-fish plotting, and multi-fish summary notebooks.

Use this file when

- The task targets `src/data_loading.py`, a narrow analysis or plotting owner, one of their compatibility facades, or `src/significant_trace_detection.py`.
- The task targets the single-fish or several-fish notebooks under `notebooks/calcium/`.
- The task mentions stimulus alignment, trial windows, reliability filtering, response types, left-right metrics, or figure behavior.

## Ownership guidance

- Exp 9 notebook 02 Cell 04c sets `DELAY_PANEL_PROPORTIONAL_WIDTHS=True`: the plotting gateway/primitive size columns by their own requested `time_bounds_s` spans, with each ending at bout offset + recovery. Raster and mean axes share limits within each column; seconds have the same physical scale across columns. Other panel callers retain the default equal-width/shared-limit layout.

- Exp 8 notebook 04's combined 3D PCA cell owns its local figure-size, axes-font and legend font/columns/location/anchor controls (`PCA_BOTH_3D_*`). `plotting_population_trajectories.plot_pooled_population_trajectories` applies these to cached paths. The distance overlay labels its unchanged common program anchor as time from stimulus motion onset.

- Consistent preconditioning pools in phase recruitment: the diagnostic owner counts passing native trials across conditioned presentations with a configurable minimum fraction (ceil). `plot_phase_recruitment_decisions` reuses `plotting_diagnostics.plot_active_trace_decision_diagnostic` for Exp 7-style mean-trace and binary-decision panels; pooled preconditioning and own-condition recruitment remain separate definitions.

- Significant phase recruitment: `build_phase_recruitment_analysis` in `src/several_fish_diagnostics.py` reuses native per-trial activity decisions, selects repetitions before counting memberships and prepares one-preconditioning-plus-bouts displays; `plot_phase_recruitment_rasters`, `plot_phase_recruitment_counts` and `plot_phase_recruitment_proportions` in `src/several_fish_figures.py` own their figures. The percentage rows divide shared C1/stimulus neurons by same-side C1-active neurons, and new-after-own-preconditioning neurons by all retained neurons in each fish, before averaging fish equally. C1 overlap and new-after-own-preconditioning are distinct memberships.

- Reviewed phase population displays: `build_phase_population_display` in `src/several_fish_diagnostics.py` selects one-based display repetitions and within-fish/pooled sorting using a fixed all-trial reference, optionally restricted by condition keys, alignment and nominal time bounds; `plot_phase_cohort_rasters` in `src/several_fish_figures.py` owns configurable raster/z-score colors, reference titles and original repetition labels. Display selection does not change phase measurements.
